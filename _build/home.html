02What Birdboost is

Birdboost is clothing for people who know they were made on purpose. We make pieces that carry a message, and we wear them together: at church, on the street, in the studio, at the next event. One bird is you. The flock is all of us.

i.Clothing

Pieces with a word on them. The Righteous tee carries Ephesians 2:10 where you can read it.

ii.Community

Built with the people who wear it. Our ambassadors carry Birdboost in their own cities.

iii.Faith

Faith first, people close, nothing fake. That's the whole brief.
